## Portfolio completion increment — 2026-11-24

Exact network/kind/issuer identities are retained. XLM includes reserved balance; all contract representations (including independent contract assets), LP and claimable balances are excluded. Missing/invalid prices and budget exclusions are disclosed as partial coverage; conflicting duplicate balances are invalid, identical duplicate balances are counted once. Exact integer products are summed before one final cent rounding. No unknown price is zero. Zero-rounded known value is valid.
